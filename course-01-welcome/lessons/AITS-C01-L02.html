Learning objective

Match customer needs to relevant service categories.

Explanation

AITS publicly lists CCTV, network cabling, security systems, internet, telephone systems, access control, home automation and solar services. Learn what each category addresses before proposing a product. CCTV supports visual monitoring; access control manages entry; network infrastructure connects devices and services.

A customer request may span several categories. Ask about the problem, location, existing equipment and desired outcome. Record uncertainty instead of promising compatibility, price, installation dates or a service level. A technical survey and approved quotation process are separate from this introductory training.

Synthetic example

Fictional Lotus Workshop wants to see its entrance remotely and restrict staff-door access. CCTV and access control are relevant; connectivity also needs investigation.

Practical exercise

List three discovery questions for Lotus Workshop without collecting passwords.

Model response

What areas need coverage? Who should be allowed through the door? What network equipment and connection already exist?

Summary

Start with the customer outcome Several services may be involved Do not promise unverified compatibility
