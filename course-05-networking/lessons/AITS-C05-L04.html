Isolation escalation and customer updates

Sample Content — Pending Company Approval

Version 0.1.0 | AITS-C05-L04 | Draft | 15 minutes

Learning objective

Hand over an investigation with evidence and a clear next action.

Explanation

Device isolation has two meanings: narrowing a fault to a device/path, and restricting a device for security containment. Do not disconnect equipment or change VLANs under the second meaning without the applicable authorization. For diagnosis, compare a known-good cable, port or client only where the test is safe and approved. Preserve the original state and record each change.

An escalation includes impact, scope, timestamps, relevant topology, tests and results, changes made, rollback status and the requested help. Exclude passwords and unnecessary personal information. Tell the customer what is known, what remains uncertain and when the next agreed update will occur. Do not promise resolution times or quote an AITS SLA that has not been verified.

Synthetic example

Synthetic update: “Two office devices cannot resolve names. Gateway checks passed. We are checking the resolver path and will update you at the agreed time.”

Practical exercise

Write a support escalation with three observations and one explicit request.

Model response

Two clients affected since 09:10; gateway reachable; configured resolver times out. No configuration changed. Please review resolver/service policy and advise the authorized next test.

Summary

One controlled comparison at a time Escalate evidence and uncertainty Promise only an agreed update
