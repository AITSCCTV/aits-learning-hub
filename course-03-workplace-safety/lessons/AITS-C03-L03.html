Height PPE and handling

Sample Content — Pending Company Approval

Version 0.1.0 | AITS-C03-L03 | Draft | 15 minutes

Learning objective

Explain why access and rescue planning precede height work.

Explanation

Consider whether work can be done from the ground. Where height work remains necessary, competent people must plan suitable access, fall prevention, equipment inspection and emergency arrangements. A harness alone does not make an unsafe setup acceptable. Ladders are not a universal solution, and a chair is not access equipment.

Select PPE for the assessed hazard, correct fit and compatibility. Inspect it and follow its instructions; damaged equipment should not be used. Do not attach fall protection to an unverified anchor. Keep people clear of falling-object areas and use approved handling arrangements. The learner’s exercise is a paper planning review; it includes no climbing, lifting experiment or live installation.

Synthetic example

A camera is above a stairwell. The safe planning response considers another mounting arrangement or properly assessed access, not balancing a ladder on a step.

Practical exercise

List the missing planning information before a fictional high-level camera installation.

Model response

Access method, competent personnel, site assessment, fall prevention, exclusion area, equipment suitability and rescue arrangements.

Summary

Avoid height exposure where possible PPE does not replace planning No climbing in this exercise
