Incidents site conduct and quality

Version 0.1.0 | AITS-C03-L04 | Draft | 15 minutes

Learning objective

Record observations and maintain professional site boundaries.

Explanation

If an incident or near miss occurs, protect yourself, seek the designated emergency response and avoid disturbing the scene except where necessary for immediate safety. Do not attempt a rescue beyond your training. Record time, location, observed conditions and witnesses through the verified reporting route. Do not speculate about blame or publish images of people.

At a customer site, confirm access permission, work boundaries and the approved scope. Protect property, keep routes clear and obtain permission before taking evidence photographs. Quality assurance means building reliable checks into the process; final inspection alone cannot repair every planning failure. Record incomplete work and unresolved risks truthfully so that the next person can act.

Synthetic example

A box falls near a colleague without injury. Treat the near miss as useful safety information rather than hiding it because nobody was hurt.

Practical exercise

Write a four-line near-miss report using fictional facts.

Model response

At 10:20 a loosely stacked box fell in aisle B. No injury observed. Area access was restricted safely. Supervisor review of stacking controls requested.

Summary

Report near misses as well as harm Respect site permissions Record unfinished work honestly
