Electrical and equipment awareness

Sample Content — Pending Company Approval

Version 0.1.0 | AITS-C03-L02 | Draft | 15 minutes

Learning objective

Recognize when equipment must be left to qualified personnel.

Explanation

Thailand has specific occupational electrical-safety regulations and training requirements; the DLPW reference index links the regulation and notices. A short online lesson is not that qualification. Do not open energized equipment, defeat guards or improvise repairs. Unexpected heat, burning smell, exposed conductors or liquid ingress require a safe stop and qualified assessment.

Network equipment can still be connected to mains supplies, backup power or other energy sources. Switching off one visible switch does not prove all energy is isolated. Isolation, verification and re-energization belong to trained authorized personnel using the approved method. Handle equipment using rated lifting and carrying methods; inspect packaging, connectors and manufacturer instructions without creating exposure.

Synthetic example

A recorder emits a burning smell. Keep people clear and seek the site emergency/qualified response; do not open the enclosure to inspect it.

Practical exercise

Sort actions into safe observation versus work requiring authorization.

Model response

Safe from a protected location: record model and reported symptom. Requires qualified authorization: opening a mains supply or proving isolation.

Summary

A network device can have hazardous energy Do not improvise electrical repairs Use qualified isolation and verification
