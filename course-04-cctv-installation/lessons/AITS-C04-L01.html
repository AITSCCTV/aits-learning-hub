Learning objective

Trace a basic CCTV signal path.

Explanation

A CCTV system combines image capture, transport, recording or storage, and authorized viewing. IP cameras send digital video over a network to an NVR or video-management system; analog cameras commonly send video to a DVR for recording. Hybrid designs exist, so verify the actual equipment rather than relying on the name alone.

A visible live image does not prove recording, time accuracy, playback or export works. A complete handover test considers the customer’s agreed purpose and approved requirements. Camera resolution alone does not guarantee identification: lens, distance, angle, lighting and motion matter. All examples here are generic; AITS-specific acceptance criteria have not been supplied.

Synthetic example

Synthetic system: two IP cameras connect to a PoE switch; the recorder and authorized viewing workstation share the permitted network.

Practical exercise

Draw the path from a camera to stored footage and identify where recording could fail while live viewing still works.

Model response

Camera → switch/network → recorder → storage. A disk or recording-schedule problem can affect storage while live video remains visible.

Summary

Capture, transport, record and view are distinct NVR and DVR describe common architectures Test playback as well as live view
