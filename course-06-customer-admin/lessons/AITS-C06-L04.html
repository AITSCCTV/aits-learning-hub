Administrative records and handovers

Sample Content — Pending Company Approval

Version 0.1.0 | AITS-C06-L04 | Draft | 15 minutes

Learning objective

Maintain accurate controlled documents and protect confidentiality.

Explanation

Use clear names, stable references and version information so another person can locate the right record. Check dates, quantities, customer identifiers and attachments against the source. A copied spreadsheet can become an uncontrolled duplicate; follow the approved storage and retention rules once supplied. Do not invent a retention period.

Restrict access according to the task, verify recipients and use approved transfer methods. A handover should state status, outstanding questions, evidence location and next owner or requested action. Do not mark a task complete merely because an email was sent. Confirm that the intended outcome or handover acceptance has been recorded by the appropriate person.

Synthetic example

Two files are named final.xlsx. In the exercise, assign a stable request reference and explicit version, then identify which version is approved.

Practical exercise

Prepare a handover for a draft quotation awaiting technical review.

Model response

CO-101 v0.2 remains Draft; six-location scope awaits confirmation. Evidence is in the authorized case folder. Technical review requested before commercial approval.

Summary

Check records against their source Use explicit versions and status Handover requires a clear next action
