Complaints and professional communication

Sample Content — Pending Company Approval

Version 0.1.0 | AITS-C06-L02 | Draft | 15 minutes

Learning objective

Acknowledge a complaint and agree a next step without unsupported promises.

Explanation

Listen without interrupting, summarize the issue and acknowledge its impact. Ask focused questions to establish what happened and what outcome the customer seeks. Avoid assigning blame before evidence is available. A respectful acknowledgement can be accurate without admitting an unverified cause or promising compensation.

Record the complaint, impact, evidence available, agreed contact method and next update. Escalate to the authorized responsible function when the request exceeds your role. Do not invent a refund rule or service deadline. If language becomes abusive or safety is threatened, seek the applicable supervisor/site response instead of escalating the confrontation.

Synthetic example

Cedar Office reports that a missed appointment interrupted work. “I understand this disrupted your schedule. I will record the details and ask the responsible team to confirm the next step.”

Practical exercise

Draft a complaint response containing acknowledgement, clarification and a realistic next action.

Model response

I understand the disruption. Please confirm the appointment reference and what occurred. I will ask the responsible team to review it and agree the next update with you.

Summary

Acknowledge impact Clarify facts without blame Agree the next update
