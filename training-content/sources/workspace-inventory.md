## Items the company must supply

Current approved organization chart and department names; employment handbook and work rules; role descriptions; leave and benefits administration; private HR/concern-reporting contacts; approved safety assessments and emergency arrangements; installation and handover acceptance criteria; vendor/model-specific manuals; quotation authority and commercial terms; data retention and privacy procedures; current SOP owners and approval records.

The source register records access dates and limits. The Ministry of Labour overview contains obsolete maternity information: only its hours/rest/holiday baseline is used. The newer government explanation of the amendment effective 7 December 2025 is used for the maternity and spouse-support summary. HR/legal review remains required before employee release.

No third-party photographs or long copyrighted passages are copied. Diagrams and fictional scenarios are original. AITS logos and internal employee/customer records are not reproduced.
