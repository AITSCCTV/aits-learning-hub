Sample Content — Pending Company Approval

Version 0.1.0 | AITS-C01-L03 | Draft | 15 minutes

Learning objective

Prepare a clear handover without inventing an organization chart.

Explanation

No current approved AITS organization chart was located in the inspected materials. This lesson therefore describes functions, not confirmed departments or reporting lines. In a typical service business, customer-facing staff capture requirements, technical staff assess feasibility, administration maintains records, and an authorized reviewer confirms commercial or operational decisions.

A useful handover states the request, known facts, open questions, relevant evidence and next action. It identifies who is being asked to respond without assuming authority they do not have. Ask your supervisor to confirm actual role names and escalation routes. A handover is incomplete when the recipient cannot tell what decision is needed.

Synthetic example

Synthetic ticket LH-001 says “camera problem.” A better handover says “Entrance camera image missing since 09:00; other views work; model and safe observations attached; technical diagnosis requested.”

Practical exercise

Rewrite “customer unhappy, please fix” as a five-field handover.

Model response

Request: restore entrance view. Facts: missing since 09:00. Evidence: authorized screenshot without people. Unknown: power status. Next action: technical review and agreed update time.

Summary

Functional map is not an organization chart Facts and unknowns travel together Confirm the real escalation route
