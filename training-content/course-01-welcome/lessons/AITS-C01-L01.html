Company identity and history

Sample Content — Pending Company Approval

Version 0.1.0 | AITS-C01-L01 | Draft | 15 minutes

Learning objective

Explain AITS using supported public facts.

Explanation

The official About us page identifies A.I.T.S. Company Limited and dates its founding to Buddhist year 2548, equivalent to 2005. It describes a business concerned with installation quality and professional service. These are public company statements; they do not establish an employee contract or a field acceptance standard.

The public mission text emphasizes service standards, technology, attention to customer needs and responsibility. In this course, these themes introduce the business. Specific targets, individual duties and performance measures require current approved internal documents. Do not infer a reporting line or an employee benefit from marketing language.

Synthetic example

A fictional new colleague asks whether the company began in 2548 or 2005. Explain the calendar conversion and identify the company page as the source.

Practical exercise

Write a two-sentence company introduction and name one fact that still requires internal confirmation.

Model response

AITS publicly dates its founding to 2005 and describes IT and security installation services. My reporting manager must be confirmed from current internal records.

Summary

Public history: founded in 2005 Service quality and responsibility are public themes Internal duties need approved records
