Recognize hazards before work

Sample Content — Pending Company Approval

Version 0.1.0 | AITS-C03-L01 | Draft | 15 minutes

Learning objective

Identify hazards and select a safe next action.

Explanation

A hazard is a source of possible harm; risk considers how harm could occur and its consequences. Before work, look for damaged cables, wet areas, unstable access, falling objects, traffic, heavy loads and blocked exits. A neat-looking site can still contain hidden services or energized equipment.

Prefer eliminating a hazard or changing the method before relying only on personal protective equipment. A beginner should report an unsafe condition and stay outside the danger area. Do not start an unfamiliar task because a customer says it is urgent. This awareness course does not replace the employer’s risk assessment, task training, supervision or site-specific controls.

Synthetic example

A loose cable crosses a busy walkway. The exercise asks for a controlled routing solution and communication, not merely a warning shouted once.

Practical exercise

Identify three hazards in a fictional storeroom and propose one safer alternative for each.

Model response

Blocked exit: clear through authorized action. Unstable stack: isolate and request safe restacking. Damaged lead: keep out of service and report.

Summary

Look for the mechanism of harm Remove or reduce exposure Pause when safe controls are missing
