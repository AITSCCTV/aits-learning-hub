Learning objective

Review power compatibility and cabling before connection.

Explanation

Power over Ethernet can deliver power and data over compatible Ethernet cabling. Check both the device’s supported PoE type and the switch or injector’s capability. Do not assume every RJ45 port supplies power or every injector is standards-compatible. Manufacturer documentation takes priority for the actual model.

A switch has per-port limits and a total power budget. Compare planned loads against the documented budget, allowing for the manufacturer’s conditions and cable losses. Axis explains that typical and maximum camera consumption differ with features and operating conditions. Inspect cable category, route, terminations, labeling, damage and suitability for the environment. Never improvise mains wiring as part of a network exercise.

Synthetic example

Four fictional cameras each require a documented maximum allocation of 15 W. Their total is 60 W; a 50 W usable budget is insufficient even before other loads.

Practical exercise

Calculate the example load and list two checks beyond total watts.

Model response

60 W exceeds 50 W. Also verify per-port support and the correct PoE type for every device.

Summary

Check PoE type and per-port limit Check total usable power budget Inspect and label the cable path
