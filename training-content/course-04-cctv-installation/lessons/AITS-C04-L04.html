Faults maintenance and handover

Learning objective

Separate symptom diagnosis from authorized corrective work.

Explanation

For a missing image, establish whether one camera or many are affected, when it began and whether live view or playback is missing. Observe authorized power/link indications and compare with a known-good path where permitted. Possible causes include power, cabling, addressing, authentication, stream compatibility and recording settings; do not jump straight to a factory reset.

Maintenance starts with approved intervals and model instructions. Review authorized health indicators, time accuracy, storage condition and image obstruction without changing settings unnecessarily. Handover evidence should identify devices, configuration versions, tests performed, results, limitations and open issues. Transfer credentials through the approved secure method, never in the ordinary completion report.

Synthetic example

Only playback is missing for camera C2; live view works. Investigate recording schedule, storage and timestamps before replacing the camera.

Practical exercise

Write a handover entry that records a passed live-view test and an unresolved playback problem.

Model response

C2 live view passed at 14:00 in the agreed scene. Playback unavailable for the requested period. Recording/storage review pending; acceptance not claimed.

Summary

Scope the symptom before changing equipment Avoid destructive resets Record results and unresolved items
