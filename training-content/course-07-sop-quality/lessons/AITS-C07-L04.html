AI checks and human authority

Sample Content — Pending Company Approval

Version 0.1.0 | AITS-C07-L04 | Draft | 15 minutes

Learning objective

Use AI feedback as practice support with source and approval controls.

Explanation

An AI can help compare a written report with a checklist, ask missing questions and explain a training example. It may still misunderstand images, omit context or produce an unsupported answer. It cannot verify hidden physical conditions from a photograph, grant approval or turn a draft SOP into company policy.

Before accepting AI feedback, inspect the cited source, version, approval and applicability. Keep company-authoritative retrieval restricted to approved company records. Draft and hypothetical items may support clearly labeled practice, but not a policy answer. Do not submit secrets or unnecessary personal data. An AI practice score is formative feedback; official competency assessment requires the company’s approved human-led process.

Synthetic example

An AI says a camera installation passes from one photograph. Ask which requirements and evidence were checked; unresolved physical tests remain unresolved.

Practical exercise

Write an AI response to “What is AITS’s travel allowance?” when no approved policy is available.

Model response

I do not have verified AITS travel-allowance terms. Please ask HR or the responsible company department for the current approved policy.

Summary

AI feedback needs source checks Draft knowledge cannot establish policy Practice scores are not certification
