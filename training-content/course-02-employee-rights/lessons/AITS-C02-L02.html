Learning objective

Recognize the ordinary statutory baseline and its limits.

Explanation

For ordinary work, Ministry of Labour guidance describes normal limits of eight hours a day and 48 hours a week, with lawful arrangements and sector exceptions requiring review. Specified hazardous work has a seven-hour daily and 42-hour weekly limit. The ordinary rest rule provides at least one hour after no more than five consecutive working hours; agreed split breaks and statutory exceptions must be checked.

The general baseline includes at least one weekly holiday with no more than six days between, at least 13 traditional holidays including National Labour Day, and at least six working days of annual holiday after one continuous year. These are legal baselines, not a statement of the AITS roster. Overtime consent, exceptions and pay should be checked separately; do not infer that a customer deadline overrides them.

Synthetic example

A fictional roster appears to schedule continuous work without a break. Identify the concern and ask HR to check the actual arrangement and exceptions.

Practical exercise

Annotate a sample 09:00–18:00 schedule with a one-hour midday break, then list what remains unknown.

Model response

Eight working hours are shown. Weekly total, holiday arrangements, actual duties and any overtime remain to be verified.
