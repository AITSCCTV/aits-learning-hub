Conduct confidentiality and concerns

Sample Content — Pending Company Approval

Version 0.1.0 | AITS-C02-L04 | Draft | 15 minutes

Learning objective

Communicate professionally and protect personal information.

Explanation

General professional practice includes accurate records, respectful communication, honesty about mistakes and protection of information entrusted to you. Collect only information needed for the legitimate task and share it only with authorized recipients through approved systems. This is privacy awareness, not a complete interpretation of Thailand’s PDPA or an AITS retention policy.

Before sending a document, check recipient, attachment, purpose and sensitivity. Never put customer passwords into a ticket or AI practice chat. For a concern, separate what you observed from what you suspect, preserve relevant evidence lawfully, and use a verified responsible channel. If the usual contact is implicated, seek an alternative authorized route. This lesson does not invent a company grievance procedure or promise a particular investigation outcome.

Synthetic example

A fictional employee receives a customer list by mistake. Stop further sharing and report the misdelivery through the verified internal route.

Practical exercise

Rewrite an accusatory message as an observation, impact and request.

Model response

The file was sent to an unintended recipient at 10:15. It contains contact details. Please advise the authorized containment and reporting steps.

Summary

Separate observation from suspicion Share only with authorized recipients Verify the route for concerns
