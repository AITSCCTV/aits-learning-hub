Leave categories and recent changes

Sample Content — Pending Company Approval

Version 0.1.0 | AITS-C02-L03 | Draft | 15 minutes

Learning objective

Locate current leave guidance and avoid outdated figures.

Explanation

Leave categories include sickness, necessary personal business, maternity, qualifying spouse support, sterilization, military service and training, each with conditions and payment rules. Sick leave and paid sick-leave limits are different concepts. Do not use an old overview as the sole source for current allowances.

The official explanation of the 2025 amendment, effective 7 December 2025, describes maternity leave of up to 120 days with employer-paid wages up to 60 days. It also describes up to 15 days of qualifying continuous child-care leave at 50% wages with the required medical evidence, and up to 15 days to assist a spouse who gives birth, paid in full, subject to the application and 90-day timing rules. These are statutory summaries, not AITS benefits. HR/legal review must confirm current text and individual eligibility before release.

Synthetic example

An older Ministry overview still says 90 maternity days. The newer official amendment explanation supersedes that figure for the relevant covered situation.

Practical exercise

Write a respectful request for current leave guidance without sharing medical details in a group chat.

Model response

Please confirm the applicable leave category, current entitlement, required documents and private submission channel for my circumstances.

Summary

Check amendment and effective date Leave duration and pay are separate Submit sensitive evidence privately
