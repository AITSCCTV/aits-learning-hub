Version 0.1.0 | AITS-C06-L01 | Draft | 15 minutes

Learning objective

Turn a customer inquiry into an actionable record.

Explanation

Begin with the outcome the customer wants and the problem they experience. Use open questions, then confirm specifics: site type, affected area, existing equipment, constraints and desired timing. Read back your understanding in plain language. Do not collect credentials or unnecessary personal data to make the record look complete.

A useful request record distinguishes customer statements from confirmed observations. Include a reference, contact channel authorized for follow-up, scope, open questions and the next responsible action. The fields in this lesson are a generic practice template; actual AITS systems and mandatory fields require company confirmation.

Synthetic example

Fictional Cedar Office says “we need better cameras.” Ask what must be visible, under what lighting and for what purpose before proposing a specification.

Practical exercise

Create an inquiry record using only synthetic details.

Model response

Request CO-101: improve entrance image under afternoon light. Existing model unknown. Survey and privacy review needed. Customer prefers follow-up through the agreed business channel.

Summary

Ask about outcome before product Read back the requirement Separate statements from verified facts
